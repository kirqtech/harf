    Case matters.

    s is س and S is ص. Same for d/D, t/T, and z/Z. Lowercase h is only ه; use H or 7 for ح.

    How scoring works

    WPM counts correct keystrokes (5 characters = 1 word) against elapsed time. Backspace fixes typos, but the original keypress still counts toward accuracy.
