    Case is part of the mapping.

    s is س while S is ص. The same applies to d/D, t/T and z/Z. Lowercase h is only ه; use H or 7 for ح.

    Choose the spelling shown in the hints. Common alternates stay accepted, so you can type the form that feels natural.

    How scoring works

    WPM counts correct keystrokes (5 characters = 1 word) against elapsed time. Backspace fixes typos, but the original keypress still counts toward accuracy.
